# SlipSurge iOS experience audit

26 November 2026 · Baseline `17b22a8e` · `codex/ios-parity-20261125`

The review indexed all 285 Swift files in the app and widget targets, then traced
the production entry points and inspected 48 files that use animation or a
redraw timeline. The native review covered navigation, touch feedback, loading,
errors, refresh, gestures, empty states, large text, permissions and role gates.
Emil Kowalski’s motion and interface criteria were applied to the existing
SwiftUI and UIKit behavior. Device evidence and source-only findings are marked
below. [Emil Kowalski’s skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills)

## Remaining findings in priority order

P1 affects data integrity or blocks an important workflow. P2 affects task
completion, accessibility or repeated use. P3 requires measurement or smaller
polish. Current locations refer to this checkpoint.
