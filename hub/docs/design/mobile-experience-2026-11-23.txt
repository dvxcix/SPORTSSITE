   above the keyboard. Bottom navigation, VOLT and resume overlays yield while
   the keyboard is open. Listeners and viewport properties are cleaned up.
10. Resume offers appear on mobile arrival or returning to the app, rather
    than after each in-app link. Cloud saving and cross-device restore remain.
