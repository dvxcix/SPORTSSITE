SlipSurge visitor journey
23 November 2026

Shape: direct offer with a working product preview before account creation.
Visitors can research a bounded public board and browse public community pages,
then choose an account or a membership when they want to participate or unlock
the existing research workspaces. No new offer, fee, upsell or pricing tier.

Inputs and direction
- Offer: existing free account and Ultimate research membership. The canonical
  pricing page currently shows $19.99 every 30 days with its existing 3-day
  trial, or $199.99 every 365 days. This update does not change billing.
- Traffic assumption: direct, organic search and social links. No new ad
  campaign, acquisition budget, affiliate program or promotional offer.
- Awareness: sports fans comparing research products, often arriving cold.
- Fulfilment: existing digital research and community access through SlipSurge.
- Existing product: both preview routes, archived public quote slices, community
  directory, Feed, creator storefronts, pricing, authentication and Whop checkout.
- Audience: someone checking the product on a phone before creating an account.
- Reading this as a sports research/community product entrance, in the existing
  SlipSurge visual language. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.
- Geist remains the product typeface. Semantic surfaces and the existing lime
  action/selection accent keep the public and member experiences connected.
- The working board is the hero focal point. Real group cards are the second
  product proof. Existing team/player artwork is preserved as supporting art.
- Short, local state transitions explain selection; no auto-cycling demos,
  floating decoration, fabricated testimonials, member counts or win claims.

Goal: open a useful research or community view without signing in.

  sport and milestone controls, and specific links to the full free previews.
Body: bounded working board; existing research/player artwork and real tool
  paths; actual public/free communities; public Feed/creator/scores destinations;
  current free/paid access distinction; product-specific questions; legal/footer.

  with original observations and official box-score source links. Community
  cards come only from a bounded public/free metadata read under normal RLS.
Objections: no account needed to preview/browse; paid spaces remain member-only;
  dated archived examples are labelled and do not imply current/live prices.
Fields: none. No lead capture or email-wall before product discovery.
Price: current canonical membership figures, with billing period/trial visible.
Exit: keep using public previews, browse community pages or read the legal/help
  pages without creating an account.
Metric: public_discovery_opened by destination, then research_preview_explored.
  Target: establish the site's own baseline before setting a conversion target;
  the skill's generic paid-traffic ranges are not a measured SlipSurge target.

Price ladder
- Entry: free anonymous research slice and public browsing.
- Free account: existing save, follow, post and joining capabilities.
- Core subscription: existing Ultimate plan, same pricing and entitlement rules.
- Creator memberships: separate published offers, unchanged creator-set pricing.
- Immediate upsell/downsell: none. The user requested discovery, not new billing.
- Back end: existing account membership management. No new campaign/nurture flow.

Instrumentation boundaries and build order
1. Verify existing preview/access/checkout contracts and public route eligibility.
2. Build the common public header and put working research above the fold.
3. Link public destinations and stream bounded public/free community metadata.
4. Add privacy-safe public_discovery_opened and public_preview_interacted events;
   event fields are destination/sport/action only, no identities or private data.
5. Root integration verifies browser flow, redirects, loading/error states,
   desktop/mobile and both themes, then publishes the single coordinated release.
6. Existing analytics do not prove ad attribution or revenue reconciliation.
   Click-ID persistence, server purchase attribution/value/currency and an A/B
   baseline are required before scaling paid traffic, not fabricated in this UI.
   No new ad pixels, provider events or purchase jobs are added by this update.
7. Future A/B slot: hero CTA wording, measured by meaningful preview interaction
   and completed account/membership action. No experiment is enabled here.

  public-browse modes, one common theme control and safe sign-in return paths.

  sport switching and milestone controls work before any account prompt.

  Root integration owns the bounded fixed-archive context resolver.
- Historical results appear only for the corresponding sourced player. Missing
  quote rows stay unavailable. Archived prices are clearly dated and labelled.
- The existing player cards, logo sources and team-color artwork were preserved.
- CommunityDirectory cards are reused with heading level 3. The homepage reads
  at most three normal-RLS public/free NBA/NFL or sport-neutral group cards,
  with a four-second abort limit and loading/empty/failure UI.
- Public destinations replace premature registration links. Sign-in/account
  entry and the current membership offer remain accessible at their real steps.
- Navbar, main and footer have separate landmarks; the skip link targets main.
- Only the selected board panel has a 160ms opacity change on sport switching;
  reduced-motion mode removes it. No auto-play, timer, carousel or floating art.
