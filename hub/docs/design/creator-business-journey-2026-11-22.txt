SlipSurge creator/member and business journey — 22 November 2026

Implemented
• Paid communities appear in discovery and creator storefronts using narrow published card metadata. Private free groups remain invitation-only.
• Pending private invitations show an entry screen instead of 404. Authenticated, actor-bound acceptance enrolls the group and channel atomically; paid invitations still require current access.
• Groups have URL-backed Posts, Picks and Live chat tabs, keyboard navigation and stable cursor pagination beyond the initial 20 posts. Group composer saves refresh the visible feed automatically. Channel mention destinations preserve their community context.
• Offers show verified status honestly and let owners open their own space. Membership completion stops polling once resolved, limits requests, prevents duplicate billing opens and explains access holds.
• Creator Studio retains saved offer fees, labels recent USD receipts accurately and exposes a closure retry for paused checkout links.
• SlipSurge admin has a searchable roster of creators and free community owners, linked communities, offers, paid-through membership status, reversible access holds, provider readiness checks, recent receipts, member administration and audit links. Legacy applications remain available.
• The fee editor previews a $20 split, requires review, supports 0–99.99%, rejects stale edits and audits changes atomically. Defaults affect newly saved offers. Existing offers and member terms retain their fee. The live default remains 10%.
• Owner/admin offer publication shares provider readiness, event delivery and linked-community checks. Pausing first closes local checkout, then provider links; failed publication attempts compensate by closing stock.
• Admin service reads require a real admin account and configured MFA assurance. Membership holds persist through later provider events; restoration rechecks the original Whop membership.
• NFL Odds Board adds verified final stat totals and Hit/Miss rung outcomes without changing frozen pregame quotes. Thursday and prior-week browser checks passed; missing stats stay unavailable. Combined passing/rushing and three-plus-touchdown grading are included.
