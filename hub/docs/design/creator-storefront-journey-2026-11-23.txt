SlipSurge creator storefront and membership journey | 23 November 2026

This replaces the previous local percentage-fee editor. Whop account fees already apply; no separate application_fee_amount is sent. No existing creator offers needed repricing: live preflight found zero products.

Implemented checklist
- workspace: Separate Overview, Products, Communities, Members, Content, Payments & settings; six visible mobile sections
- products: Details, Pricing, Review; 7/30/90/365-day recurring or finite one-time access; durable draft retries and publication guards
- fees: No additional application_fee_amount; local fee retired; provider fee schedule read-only in business administration
- connection: Admin connection uses the runtime credential/account fallback and an actual account health check
- branding: Creator banner and profile identity; independent community banner/avatar editing in Studio and onboarding
- discovery: Branded free/paid community cards, search and sport filters; membership options lead the storefront
- restore: Verified Whop identity restores only the exact child-account product membership; active paused-product members retain an entry point
- members: Searchable paid-member roster with effective expiry status; retained invitations, community roles and channels
- discord-setup: Verified Discord identity and server management; bot hierarchy and safe role mapping; creator-specific validated invite
- discord-access: Whop verification before paid role delivery, admin holds, expiry, identity unlink, community deletion, durable retry and lease/version fencing
- content: Posts, Picks and Live chat retained; content calendar in its own area; private post/chat and publishing journey exercised
- release: Canonical GitHub main, Ready Vercel production, live desktop/mobile Light/Dark checks
