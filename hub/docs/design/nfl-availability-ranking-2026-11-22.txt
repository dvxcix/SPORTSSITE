NFL availability and ranking repair — 22 November 2026

Scope
The Sideline, its player drawers, NFL Odds Board, Cheatsheets, Matchup Lab,
The Public, Sportsbooks and the NFL Slate Edge ranking pool.

Implemented
- Removed the nested one-hour HTTP cache from game-specific NFL designation
  requests. The separate reference-statistics cache remains unchanged.
- Game reports are requested with season, week, season phase and matchup team
  IDs. Every page must complete; repeated or exhausted pagination fails closed.
- Pregame reports and enriched boards refresh through 15-second shared caches.
  Existing visible/online client polling runs about every 30 seconds.
- Out, inactive, suspended, reserve, practice-squad and invalid roster identities
  cannot lead eligible player rankings or Most hidden/Most advertised cards.
- Questionable and Doubtful remain distinct from Out. Game-day Active supersedes
  an earlier Questionable designation. Contradictory explicit Out remains blocked.
  A body-part note alone is labelled Status unconfirmed, never assumed Out/Active.
- Ranked rows need a usable current quote for their score's market. Opening-only,
  malformed and absent prices cannot qualify. MM compares the same priced pool.
- Unlisted and unavailable players sit in separate collapsible sections below
  current-market players. Research drawers and historical production remain
  reachable. Withdrawn status metadata contains no odds, baselines or pick counts.
- Drawer headers retain game status, injury reason and reported time across tabs.
  Shared NFL player cards display the game designation too.
- Price-history sliders retain this game's latest permitted status evidence;
  a withdrawn player cannot return through an older price frame.
- Odds Board rechecks open-game availability after cached quote merges. A cached
  price or successful game-clock poll cannot renew an expired injury report.
- An open game's report expires after 90 seconds without a verified observation.
  Current player prices/ranks are withheld with a clear message. Existing valid
  game lines remain governed by their separate quote validation.
- Cheatsheets and Matchup Lab now poll the shared matchup feed. Public/Sportsbooks
  and The Sideline use the same independent client-side availability expiry.
- Slate Edge omits unavailable/unquoted players and requires the same market pool
  for production and sportsbook ranks.

Historical integrity
Default pregame research, scores, MM and peer pools still freeze at the earliest
known kickoff. Current roster/injury reports are not substituted into past games.
No collector/job replay, new scheduled job, migration replay, payment change,
feature flag change or secret change was performed.

Verification

Source limit

Unknown designations stay explicit. Practice participation is never treated as a
game-day inactive designation. Missing historical evidence is not invented.
