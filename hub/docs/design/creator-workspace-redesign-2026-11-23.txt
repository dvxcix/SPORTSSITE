Implemented checklist
[x] Dedicated Creator Studio navigation: Overview, Products, Communities, Members, Content, Analytics, Balance & payouts, Settings. The research sidebar does not occupy the business workspace.
[x] Separate customer shell for creator discovery, storefronts, offers and membership returns. Discover, communities, account, Creator Studio and appearance remain reachable.
[x] Products catalogue with search, status filter, explicit drafts, price/access period, linked community and publication actions. Existing pause, resume and closed-link verification remain available.
[x] Focused product dialog: details, pricing, review and live member preview. Closing and reopening retains the unsaved draft while the page stays mounted. Request IDs and exact saved-draft retries remain stable after provider failures.
[x] Free or paid community creation in a dialog, including banner/avatar upload and product binding. Successful creation returns to Studio rather than taking creators out of their workspace.
[x] Community appearance, Discord and management sections in a focused dialog. Channels, invitations, roles, member management, community posts and chat remain linked to their existing permission-checked tools.
[x] Public profile editor in Studio for creator name, bio, avatar and cover. It updates only the signed-in member's profile fields; existing effects, rings, social links and preferences are preserved.
[x] Branded discovery cards with creator covers/avatars, search and sorting by followers, price or name. No invented sales, testimonials, reviews or revenue.
[x] Storefront cover/identity, membership cards, direct Join action, existing-member/owner access links, membership details and community discovery.
[x] Whop Elements checkout dialog with actual account-scoped server-authored checkout configuration, member attribution, light/dark appearance and validated hosted fallback.
[x] Checkout configuration rejects missing or malformed configuration IDs and untrusted destinations. Owner, already-entitled, paused, readiness and rate gates remain enforced on the server.
[x] Failed/canceled checkout returns show a retry path. An active server-verified entitlement wins over return-page wording. Browser callbacks and URL status never grant membership or Discord roles.
[x] Content calendar keeps scheduled posts and drafts visible; creating/editing a post uses a focused dialog with audience, community destination and schedule controls. Provider errors remain visible inside that dialog.
[x] Analytics uses readable labels, ordinary case, consistent surfaces, responsive metrics, funnel and membership performance table. No scoring, financial aggregation or membership policy changed.
[x] Mobile account actions retain My Picks and Watchlist portal hosts. VOLT remains the only floating launcher, underneath portaled dialogs.
[x] Dialogs contain keyboard focus, close on Escape when idle, restore focus, and prevent dismissal during saves. Phone editors fill the viewport with one scrollable body. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Testing boundary

No live membership purchase, financial-account creation, KYC acceptance, payout, role grant or member message was performed for QA. The published checkout and fulfillment contracts are covered by provider-response and database transaction tests; this is not a receipt for a real paid customer transaction.
