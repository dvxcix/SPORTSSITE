SlipSurge self-service creator communities — 22 November 2026

Scope and owner decision
Free hosted communities are available to signed-in members. Paid creators begin Whop setup themselves, with no SlipSurge application approval. Whop verification and payment capabilities remain mandatory. The configured 10% creator platform fee is preserved. Existing research plans, main/add-on billing, collected research, member tiers, sports navigation and historical creator applications remain intact.

Implemented
- Creator Studio is the launch destination for every signed-in member. The old application URL redirects there.
- A three-step launch panel separates community creation, Whop verification and offer publication. Completion reflects actual state.
- Subaccounts are linked to the configured SlipSurge Whop parent and stable external user identity. Repeated onboarding resumes the same account. Existing admins keep their role; member research tiers are unchanged.
- Current, pinned Whop REST contracts are isolated from existing main/add-on billing. Hosted onboarding and the payout portal retain identity checks, balances, payout methods, withdrawals and billing management.
- Draft offers cannot accept payments. A ready connected account, correctly configured child-event delivery and an owned member community are required to publish. Pausing closes new checkout access and existing Whop links, while preserving current members.
- Checkout binds the creator, offer and authenticated buyer. Returning from checkout waits for verified membership delivery before unlocking.
- Membership events re-fetch provider state, validate account/plan/user ownership and apply access atomically. Duplicate payment events do not double-count sales. Stale activations cannot override a newer expiry.
- Paid groups, channels, posts, comments, polls, votes and social reactions follow current access. Saved group/channel membership does not bypass expiry.
- Free group owners can draft and schedule content for their own communities. The existing cloud publisher retains the destination. Legacy unbound premium drafts fail closed. No job was replayed or duplicated.
- Owners keep member preference controls without an automatic member welcome prompt. Entitled buyers can rejoin after leaving. Leaving a group explains that billing is managed separately.
- Private/paid group composers say Members. Paid settings explain membership access. Community chat, avatar and banner inputs have accessible names.
- Studio, payouts, marketplace, storefront, offers, groups, settings and access completion use the existing desktop/mobile and light/dark shell. The 320px channel-type picker wraps instead of overflowing.
