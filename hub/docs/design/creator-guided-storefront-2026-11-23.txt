research, memberships, earnings and discovery inside SlipSurge. The owner

Implemented:
- Animated, keyboard-contained setup dialog: identity/branding, sports/tags,
  free or paid membership, embedded Whop verification when paid, and preview.
- Durable draft and resume. Edits to a published identity remain private
  until Publish. New member communities stay private until publication.
- Store name, cover, logo and bio are separate from the member profile.
  Community summaries respect their shorter limits without losing the bio.
- Free stores join creator discovery. Sport logos, tags and search expose
  coverage. SlipSurge admins can feature published stores and set placement;
  those controls are protected by server authorization, MFA and audit logs.
- Posts & research opens the existing rich composer for public or private
  community posts, picks, media and research. Scheduling is a secondary tool.
- Public storefronts include original research previews and member access.
- Native balance, gross revenue, payout history, currency/status filters and
  details. Withdrawals, bank setup and verification use Whop Elements in
  dialogs on SlipSurge. No new application fee or fee percentage is added.
