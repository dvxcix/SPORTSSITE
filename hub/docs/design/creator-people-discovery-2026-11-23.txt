SlipSurge creator discovery: people, storefronts and communities
23 November 2026
Implementation handoff. Final build and browser verification belong to release integration.

Problem corrected
The creator directory and public creator page replaced a member's display name, bio, avatar and banner with storefront branding. The directory also discarded their actual avatar ring and led with community/price language. A person and their business became the same entity on screen.

Result
- /creators is a directory of people. Cards use the actual public member name, username, avatar, ring, banner, bio and verified flag.
- Published storefront name/logo is a separate, labeled context below the person. It never replaces the person.
- Creator profile and public posts/record are distinct destinations. The storefront action opens the memberships section of the creator's public page. /groups remains the communities directory.
- Sports coverage retains the existing real sports logos. Search matches member identity, public coverage/tags, store terms and active membership terms. Featured/follower/name/price sorting remains available, with missing-price entries handled without invented prices.
- The public creator page uses the member's own header and includes a direct member-profile link. Store cover/logo/bio live in a distinct storefront section. Existing offers, checkout and access actions are unchanged.
- Published free creators remain discoverable without a paid product. A published free storefront is now valid evidence to display its owner publicly even when no group/product is readable; unpublished ordinary users still hit the existing not-found guard. No creator role or payment eligibility changes.
- Absent bios/counts/cover images do not become invented member content or statistics. No paid/free membership success was claimed or triggered.

Visibility and operational boundaries
Directory sources still require published storefront status, active nested offers and the existing creator/admin role requirement on the legacy active-product source. No new account enumeration source was added. Profile blocking, request-scoped queries, product publication, group access, current entitlements, off-season group filtering and checkout behavior are preserved. No RLS, schema, provider, membership, fee, account-role or backend mutations were made.

Design brief
Human: a visitor choosing whose sports research to follow, before deciding whether to join any community or membership.
Focal point: the creator's actual face/ring and name; store branding and offers follow as context.
Domain: people, public posts, records, sports coverage, storefronts, creator memberships, communities.
Palette: existing light/dark SlipSurge canvas, surface, ink, border and lime selection tokens; real sports marks retain their source colors.
Signature: member avatars with their own rings beside actual sport coverage, not a store logo masquerading as a person.
Rejected defaults: storefront as identity; price as the dominant person-card value; invented/filler biography text.
Density: 20px card padding, 16px content rhythm, 64px person avatars and 44px actions. Three/two/one card columns follow available width. No new motion, font, imagery or dependencies.
