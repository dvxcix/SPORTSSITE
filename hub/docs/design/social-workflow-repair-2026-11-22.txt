Social workflow repair — 2026-11-22

Changes
- Original badge artwork fits inside its thumbnail; long descriptions wrap.
- Feed, Community, Messages and Picks stay above the scrolling research tools.
- The header has compact search and Feed, Groups, Live rooms and Messages links.
  Phones retain one 44px Messages shortcut, the Community dock and the full menu.
- Channel history names both sender foreign keys and selects the latest messages.
  Direct and group conversation history also selects the latest bounded page.
- Confirmed channel sends appear immediately without depending on realtime echo.
  Channel reconnect and tab-return recover missed history; retry preserves drafts.
- Direct-message read notifications and accepted-conversation writes now execute.
- New-message search queries all permitted recipients instead of 50 suggestions.
  Blocked users and members with DMs disabled remain excluded. Search can retry;
  selected group members remain visible while searching for the next person.
- Admins can reach group creation under the same permissions as the live RPC.
  Owned and joined groups remain accessible separately from public discovery.
- Failed inbox, channel, membership and group-history reads show recovery states.
  Interrupted creation or image saving no longer silently reports full success.

Verification
- 48 browser receipts: 12 social routes in desktop/phone and light/dark.
- Four additional 320px route checks; no horizontal overflow or broken visible images.
- Actual private UI exercised channel send/edit/reply/soft-delete, group chat
  send/edit/reply, recipient search, selected-member chips and direct-message send.
- 42 focused regression tests and 134 production source checks passed.
- Changed production TypeScript/React lint passed. Optimized local build and typecheck passed.
- Real authenticated database roles passed rollback-only group create/join,
  channel send, direct send, group conversation create/send and history reads.
  Private groups/channels stayed hidden from nonmembers. Follow-up verified no
  test groups, conversations or messages remained. No schema or flags changed.
- NBA tools, Odds Board and NFL tools remain reachable from the full menu and
  mobile research picker. Existing tier, paid-community and privacy rules remain.

Limits
PC browser viewport verification, not physical phone certification. No messages
or invitations were delivered to real members for QA. The private preview uses
separate browser/server fixture stores; new-group handoff is also covered by
component tests plus real database creation/read checks inside a rolled-back
transaction. Those checks are not a real cross-account delivery test.

Live follow-up: the first deployment exposed PGRST200 on the reply-to self
relationship in the live REST schema cache. Cache refresh alone did not resolve
it. Channel, DM and group replies now use separate context-scoped, ordinary-RLS
queries. Deleted or inaccessible reply bodies are omitted. No grants were widened.
