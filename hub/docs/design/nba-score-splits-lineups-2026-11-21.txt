SlipSurge NBA score, public splits and game-specific lineups
2026-11-21

Scope
- Repaired the shared radial with contained SVG geometry, a real zero state, finite bounds and accessible values. Compact tables use 52px dials; calculation drawers use 78px dials.
- DvP and Score drawers show the selected market/contract, final numeric score, six weighted inputs, input coverage, original observation time, workload effect and market adjustment. Slate uses the same drawer.
- Version nba-opportunity-market-v4 feeds market-specific recent workload into the existing opportunity (35%) and minutes (20%) inputs. Form (20%), stability (10%), matchup (10%) and pace (5%) retain their weights. The existing selected-quote movement/ladders adjustment remains in use.
- Availability uses definite Out/Inactive/Suspended teammates with recent team participation in the complete roster, including teammates without the selected contract. It is bounded at x1.25. Questionable/Doubtful are not treated as definite absences; an absence already reflected in the two recent team games is not counted twice. This is a descriptive score input, not invented rotation minutes or a hit probability.
- Combined markets aggregate their matching production units. Double/triple-double use geometric PTS/REB/AST workload support only when every component is observed, without inventing a joint probability. Missing category inputs and unsupported quarter/other workload markets remain explicit. Per-market scores retain their independent production history and market signals.
- Board, Matrix, Slate and future existing research captures use the same prepared full-roster workload context. No cron/job was replayed. Existing archived score rows are not rewritten. Recovered legacy Slate rows preserve their original score and explicitly lack a reconstructed breakdown.
- Fixed frozen pace provenance: valid saved same-season pre-tip ratings remain usable after tip. Missing/wrong-season/post-tip ratings remain unavailable. Explicit Live may retrieve current team ratings and injuries under its own board cache key.
- Public game cards show Bets and Money simultaneously. Team bars use primary/secondary palettes with a primary-color floor for visibility; Total uses SlipSurge green/red. Missing measures are not derived from the other measure or fabricated complements. Public aggregates are not individual sportsbook ticket counts.
- Pregame lineups use the provider's separate projected/confirmed source and original updated_at. Tonight's PG/SG/SF/PF/C slots are displayed with portraits and names. Expected never becomes confirmed merely because tip passed. Actual game-row positions are restricted to explicit Live. Missing historical lineups say No pre-tip lineup recorded.
- Phone team panels stack and retain five usable lineup cards. No tools removed, no entitlement changes.

Source evidence
Official BallDontLie NBA documentation: https://nba.balldontlie.io/#projected-lineups
Projected lineups stop updating at scheduled start or earlier live state and retain their last pregame record. Actual /v1/lineups is available only after a game begins. The implementation validates game/team, five unique identities/slots, original source time and the earliest known cutoff. Late retrievals are identified separately from captures before tip.
