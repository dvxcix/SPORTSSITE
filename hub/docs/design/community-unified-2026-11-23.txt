Community membership and social navigation, 23 November 2026

Scope: one place for a member to find and open each creator's community,
then reach posts, picks and member chat. Retain the SlipSurge theme, sports
tools, existing memberships, content, separate access levels and all routes.
The owner requested consolidation of duplicate creator/group entries and
removal of the eleven-item social bar. This is a targeted repository change.

The baseline showed two ParlayParty cards. The store wizard automatically
created a free draft space although the creator already owned a paid space.
They have different access rules; combining their membership data would be
incorrect. No group, post, chat message or entitlement is deleted or merged.

Changes:
1. One creator card contains its accessible spaces. Joined creators appear
   once; unjoined access levels remain reachable inside that same card.
   Filters choose a matching space and open its actual destination. Member
   totals are not summed across spaces, since people can belong to both.
2. Your communities and Discover are separate local views, with compact
   search/filter controls and a direct member-chat shortcut. Invitations stay
   reachable within their creator's entry and do not imply membership.
3. Feed, Communities, Live rooms and Messages remain the core navigation.
   Desktop uses the existing top bar. Mobile has a compact local row. Saved,
   Activity and the less-used sections move to More, with anchored placement
   and Escape dismissal. Community redirects to Groups, so there is one hub.
4. Store setup can reuse owned communities with exactly matching membership
   access. Ambiguous choices require selection. Published access cannot be
   switched in a branding draft; unpublished free/paid drafts can change
   without deleting the previously created space. Linking does not mutate an
   existing group's brand, content, privacy or memberships. Publication is
   still the deliberate branding/access action. Legacy create links open
   Creator Studio and retain a validated owned product context.
5. Group Posts/Picks/Chat tabs use the same spacing and button treatment.
   Channel names no longer repeat internal type labels. A missing chat has
   an explicit unavailable state and an owner settings link, rather than
   silently selecting Posts. Owner/moderator avatar marks fit their circles.
