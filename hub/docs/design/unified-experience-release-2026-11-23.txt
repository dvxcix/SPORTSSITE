SlipSurge unified experience release
23 November 2026

Owner-authorized scope
One coordinated navigation/social, visitor-discovery and admin release. Applied
antislop, sales-funnel-blueprint and interface-design to the existing product.
The approved direction is the existing Geist, semantic light/dark surfaces and
lime action/selection accent. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1; the public working
board uses local selection transitions (MOTION 2), never an auto-cycling demo.

Privacy and operational boundaries

creator restrictions and public boolean feature responses. No RLS, grant,
membership, price, Whop fee or feature-flag mutation belongs to this release.
No sports models, formulas, collector schedules, team/player assets or odds
calculation were changed. Existing source history and owner approvals remain.
Browser coverage is viewport emulation, not physical-device certification.
No real messages, posts, charges, payout/KYC actions, destructive admin actions
or job replays were performed to test the interface. Field speed/conversion and
provider delivery are not claimed as measured outcomes of this design review.
