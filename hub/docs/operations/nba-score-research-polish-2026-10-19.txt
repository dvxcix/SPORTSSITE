Internal scoring contract
nba-opportunity-market-v3 preserves the established opportunity/minutes/form/
stability/matchup/pace score and its minimum sample requirements. One bounded
0–8 research-priority adjustment may apply to a selected over or milestone quote.
This is descriptive research context, not a calibrated win probability, demonstrated
betting profit, or an inference about a bookmaker's intent.

The target's exact same-book contract must lengthen by at least 0.25 implied
probability points while a mapped related category at that book shortens by at
least 0.25 points. The related category must have at least 10 more observed Pikkit
picks; combined category counts must be at least 20. Counts represent categories,
not dollars or side-specific allocations. Under receives no inferred public boost.

Adjustment = 8 × clamp(min(target lengthening, related shortening)/5)
 × clamp((related share - .5)/.35) × clamp(combined picks/100)
 × related picks/(related picks + strongest opposing category's picks).
Each clamp is 0..1. Round adjustment to 0.1; retain strongest pair only; round and
clamp final score to 0..100. Additional correlated supporting categories never stack.
Defensive events/turnovers have no unsupported mappings; quarter markets only pair
within the same quarter. A production score remains required.

The displayed current price must match the evidence's game/player/book/market/type/
threshold/side. Verified price observations expire after 15 minutes and public
observations after 90 minutes. Public evidence cannot postdate the price observation.
Historical context freezes before tipoff. Future/mismatched/stale evidence contributes
nothing. A warm shared cache and outer Slate cache cannot prolong a boost.

Operational safeguards
market-attempt:v3 receipts include successful player-quote fingerprints and observed
times. A successful game-line capture alone cannot freshen an older player-price tape.
Public receipts similarly bind to the selected capture fingerprint. Shared context
loads once per game, without provider reads or database calls per player/cell.
Research archives use the same formula and exact reference; polling-only verification
timestamps are excluded from deduplication identity while provenance remains stored.
Defense failures preserve production-only scores. Customer responses and browser
components omit scoring weights, model reports, error metrics and training details.
