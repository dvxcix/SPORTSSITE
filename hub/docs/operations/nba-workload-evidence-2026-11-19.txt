Problem
The NBA Score panel exposes past production and market signals, but not a directly inspectable opportunity calculation for the selected market. The existing numeric score uses shots for PTS/3PM and primarily per-36 production for REB/AST. A descriptive workload calculation should be visible without implying it is a validated hit selector.

Change
The final UI reuses the existing PaintScoreCell / MechanicsScoreRing, including all size and color thresholds. There is no additional Workload column or sorting. Click or tap the existing radial score to open its score details, containing:
- Last-two and earlier opportunities and minutes; source sample sizes.
- Recent production, earlier conversion, and the explicit estimate.
- Gap to the exact selected target with correct Over/Under direction.
- Full-roster team workload rank/share, and saved input timestamp in Eastern time.
- An explicit warning that passes are a proxy, not potential assists.
- Missing evidence as unavailable, including missing historical tracking.

Formula
Last two prior appearances' mean opportunities multiplied by sum(production)/sum(opportunities) in the earlier available three-to-eight appearances. Uses at most ten prior current-team/current-season completed full-game appearances.
PTS: FGA + 0.44 FTA (shot equivalents).
3PM: three-point attempts.
REB: recorded rebound_chances_total.
AST: recorded passes, explicitly labelled a proxy.
The estimate describes continuation of recent workload; it does not forecast an injury-adjusted rotation.
Confirmed out/doubtful/inactive/suspended and questionable/game-time-decision players are excluded from team-rank/share pools. The player's own descriptive sample remains inspectable.

Freeze and access
Tracking is loaded through existing getNbaResearchTracking inside resolveNbaResearchBundle. A tip-crossing request resolves the final board and final tracking together. No new tables, schedules, jobs or providers. Missing tracking does not hide independent existing scores/odds or substitute today's values for historical inputs. The response retains the existing access gate and private/no-store policy.
Current/future outcomes, past seasons, previous teams, quarter rows, conflicting tracking and invalid identities cannot enter the new calculation.

Validation and limits
The retrospective estimate audit covered 122 games. Mean absolute errors versus the recent-production-average reference:
PTS 5.0885 vs 4.7327 (2,313 observations)
REB 2.0855 vs 1.9165 (2,270)
AST 1.4822 vs 1.3954 (2,274)
3PM 1.0596 vs 1.0062 (2,146)
All four estimates were less accurate than that reference. These previously studied games are development evidence, not pristine independent selection validation.
Therefore this change does NOT add an arbitrary numeric score boost, replace the validated projection or MM, or advertise a winner selector. It integrates inspectable workload evidence into the public Score panel. Numeric weighting and slate forecasting remain separate work requiring successful validation.
