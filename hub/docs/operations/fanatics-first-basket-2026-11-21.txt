Fanatics First Basket independent refresh — 2026-11-21

The owner authorized correcting Fanatics delays while retaining strict stale
quote blocking, full-flow tests and production publication.

The five-book request had a 60-second shared lease, followed by a 30-second
phase aggregate and 60-second visible refresh. A 60-second cloud attempt TTL
could also skip the next minute's dispatch when the prior write finished a few
seconds after the minute boundary. These extra waits delayed recovery after a
bookmaker began publishing fresh market observations again.

Fanatics now uses its own shared 30-second request for First Basket and team
First Basket. The other four books retain their shared 60-second request.
Both sources load concurrently, with exact event, book and market validation.
An outage in one source leaves the other source usable and explicitly marks
coverage incomplete; it cannot become a full-feed withdrawal. Empty invalid
or expired panels also fail completeness. There are no extra browser sessions,
new collectors, paid-plan changes, schema changes or historical replays.

The phase aggregate uses 15 seconds, the visible First Basket view refreshes
every 30 seconds, and the cloud attempt expires after 45 seconds so each
scheduled minute can inspect it. Member traffic shares the same source leases.
No per-member provider loop or manual refresh bypass was added. This uses the
existing Odds API plan: one four-book/three-market request per 60 seconds and
one Fanatics/two-market request per 30 seconds per active matched game/phase.

Original bookmaker market timestamps remain untouched. Future observations,
prices older than five minutes and phase-incompatible quotes remain blocked.
The final slate-response check still runs after movement history completes.
Suspended or closed upstream markets cannot be converted to current prices by
refreshing a request. They recover automatically when a fresh source market
observation arrives. The provider documents these market timestamp semantics:
https://the-odds-api.com/liveapi/guides/v4/

Validation: 762/762 NBA tests and 124/124 freeze tests passed. New fixtures
verify independent recovery at 31 seconds without re-fetching the other books,
25 concurrent readers sharing requests, exact original clocks, book outages,
stale/future observations, wrong events and protection against false full-feed
withdrawals. TypeScript and focused ESLint passed. No VOLT backend is included.

Live preflight at 12:26 UTC found current Fanatics prices for all six games;
market timestamps ranged from 36 to 272 seconds old. This establishes current
availability separately from the new refresh behavior. Release and post-release
evidence is recorded in the local docs/design/evidence/live/fanatics-* files.
