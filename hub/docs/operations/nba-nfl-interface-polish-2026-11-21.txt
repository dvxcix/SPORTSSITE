Changes
- Plain tooltips now show a compact label instead of inferred decorative cards.
  Authored metric, player and book detail cards retain their actual information.
  Pointer travel across the tooltip gap works; Escape dismisses the hint before
  its parent drawer. Native touch controls activate without a hover overlay.
- Court market format, outcome and target controls share a compact disclosure.
  The same controls remain available in the mobile filter drawer.
- First Basket has one sportsbook selector for one or several books. Secondary
  movement filters are collapsed, retain their values and show a filtered state.
  Game, team, market, sorting and player search remain directly available.
- NBA date navigation and NBA profile favorites have 44px phone targets.
  NFL player market save buttons have 44px phone targets and matching cell space.
- NBA comparison, NFL slate ranking, snap-count and NFL profile tables expose
  named, keyboard-focusable scroll regions. Existing sticky columns and table
  scrolling remain intact. Removed redundant Signal Lab reading instructions.
- The private audit can select the full NBA/NFL inventory. Its health receipts
  include actual URLs, active dialogs, button names and table scroll access.
  Audit routes stay unavailable without the private local build flag.
- Checklist generation retains VOLT and independently verified release history.
  Validation checks release ancestry rather than requiring all surfaces to have
  the same original publication date.

Limits and invariants
This is browser viewport verification, not physical-device certification.
Reduced-motion guards were retained; an OS motion-setting switch was not tested.
The illustrative fixtures do not certify live source completeness or latency.
No database mutation, collector replay, scoring formula, quote freshness rule,
pregame cutoff, access rule, payment behavior or AI backend was changed.
The unrelated older MLB/Dugout contract suite still has its two recorded
baseline source assertions; this release does not claim to certify that suite.
