The v2 writer encodes existing v1 pool inputs losslessly and keeps both the

3,667,049 to 1,587,614 bytes; one previously saved full-pool sample shrank
from 4,493,835 to 2,445,066 bytes. Actual savings vary by slate.

Once v2 has been written, roll back only to a release that includes the v2
reader, such as the verified reader release above. A v1-only deployment cannot

Do not repair that by filling historical values with current data. During the
transition retain v1 reader support for previously saved snapshots.

presence of this document or passing local tests does not prove either.
