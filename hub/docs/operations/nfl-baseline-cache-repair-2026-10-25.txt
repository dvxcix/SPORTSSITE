and slate-entry cache nest the daily touchdown baseline reader. Consequently

GETs from 16:54 to 16:59 UTC. Indexed first/final-page queries took 0.659/8.14 ms;
there is no proven slow-index cause for the separate client-side timeouts.

Repair
Reuse the existing service-only shared cache and cross-instance refresh lease,
with isolated nfl:td-baselines:v1:<date> keys. Recent dates retain 300-second TTL,
historical dates seven days; stale fallback is bounded to 300 seconds beyond
expiry and keeps the original fetched_at. No new table, migration, collector,
schedule, secret or provider call. No account/tier information is shared.
The complete source load is bounded to 40 seconds, with existing 500-row pages,
stable identity order and 50,000-row ceiling. Any page or missing-table failure
preserves the prior complete payload; it cannot cache a false empty result.
Legitimately empty dates remain cacheable. Existing pregame load callbacks and
durable cutoff checks remain unchanged. Closed games do not fetch this cache or
today's baselines. The enriched board cache key advances to the new implementation.
