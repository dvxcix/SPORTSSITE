NBA odds movement completion — October 19, 2026

Morning audit found current-only NBA prices on the research board, sportsbook
grid, ladders, player panels, cheatsheets, Score/DvP, Slate Edge and game markets.
All now use a shared NOW / OPEN / signed delta component. Green/down arrow means
shortening odds; red/up arrow means lengthening odds, consistent with NFL. The
numeric delta is implied-probability percentage points, continuous across even
money. Color indicates market direction, not a winning prediction.

OPEN is SlipSurge's first captured quote for the exact game, player, sportsbook,
market, contract type, threshold and side. It is not a guaranteed sportsbook
launch price. Unknown history remains a dash. Saved picks retain their original
selection price rather than being silently rewritten as a current quote.

player-contract openings across three opening games and fifteen quoted markets.
Late-listed alternatives use their own first observation, even beyond Market

after the selected capture. Cross-market signals can now include contracts
first listed after the game's baseline snapshot.

immutable exact-threshold opening records. Earlier game opening prices were not
archived; they are not invented. Their comparison begins with the new collector's
first successful capture. A->B->A price changes are retained. Failure of one quote
source or archive does not discard the healthy source's successful capture.

Movement selects the same book's own balanced reference; Q1 milestone fallback
