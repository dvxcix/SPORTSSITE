  only; game-specific positions and original source timestamps are preserved.
- Source errors and failed durable saves remain unavailable. A partial feed or
  stale sibling cannot erase the last complete market frame, become a withdrawal
  or certify full coverage. Missing lineups are never guessed or promoted.
