ThePaint NBA research parity audit — 2026-10-19

Scope: compare ThePaint with TheSideline and TheDugout, complete NBA research
tools and visual flows, retain hidden/admin-only access. Owner explicitly
selected Pikkit as NBA public-pick source. Existing MLB/NFL jobs untouched.

Compared implementation:
- Sideline: core/props/usage/tracking/team/custom boards, player detail,
  matchup lab, cheatsheets, sportsbook comparison, public pick counts,
  market story, save/post/grading, historical context.
- Dugout: signal/market/props/power/custom views, recency windows, MM,
  observed mechanics/Statcast, player inspectors, comparisons, public counts,
  odds/history, save/post, weather/park context, persistent matrices.

NBA tools and data boundaries:
- ThePaint: original research/odds/ladders/movement preserved.
- Cheatsheets: every available supported player-market reference, book and
  side, same full-roster MM pool, L5 average, L10 hit rate, side-adjusted
  L10-minus-line, positive/negative MM filters, exact-line save actions.
- Usage/tracking: sortable L5/L10/L20 minutes/FGA/3PA/FTA/points per36;
  non-overlapping minutes change; actual season usage, potential assists,
  rebound chances and drives. Season tracking is separately labeled and
  deliberately not relabeled as an L5/L10/L20 tracking sample.
- Matchup Lab: team pace/offensive/defensive/net ratings, true shooting,
  rebound share, home/road context, clickable rotation sorted by minutes.
- Availability: injuries/description/return date and confirmed starters,
  injury-only filter, clickable players; absence from injury list is not
  represented as confirmed availability.
- Game markets: each book's moneyline, spread with its price, total O/U
  with its price. Values are never silently aligned across different lines.
- ThePublic: Pikkit player-market counts, shares within matched market,
  prior-capture count change, filters/search and player drilldown. Counts
  are explicitly not wager dollars or exact threshold/side volumes.
- Per-player trends, shooting zones, combined markets, H2H/home/away,
  comparisons, weighted matrix, saved picks, posting and exact grading
  remain integrated. Selected sportsbook now applies throughout panels.

Do not claim literal NFL/MLB parity: NFL NGS/plays/TD routes, baseball
Statcast/pitch mechanics, weather and park factors are sport-specific.
NBA has observed stats and market ranks, not a fitted NBA win-probability
model or a synthesized player-specific defense-vs-position forecast.
Older NBA line history is not backfilled with invented observations.
